## Harbor Club membership

Joining the Harbor Club is free. All enrolled members pay **10% below the
listed price** on every order, storewide, capped at **$20.00** of
discount per order. Membership pricing is applied automatically at
checkout once your account is linked.

## Gold tier service benefits

Customers who reach Gold status enjoy, on top of member pricing:

- An **extra 12 months of warranty coverage** on all electronics
  purchases, added to the standard manufacturer warranty at no charge.
- A **free upgrade to express shipping** on any order — normally a
  **$9.95** surcharge at checkout.

## Returns

Items may be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund,
provided they are unworn/unopened with tags attached. Return shipping is
free via prepaid label.

## Shipping

Standard shipping is free on orders over **$35** (otherwise **$4.95**)
and arrives in 2-5 business days. Express upgrade available at checkout
for **$9.95**. We ship Mon-Fri.
