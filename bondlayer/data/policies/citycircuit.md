## Returns

Change of mind returns accepted within **30 days** where the product is unopened
and in original condition. Proof of purchase required. Return postage is at the
customer's expense unless the item is faulty.

Opened software, consumables and clearance items are not returnable.

## Warranty

Products are covered by the manufacturer's warranty. CityCircuit does not
provide additional cover. Warranty claims are directed to the manufacturer in
the first instance; we will assist with lodgement but do not handle repairs
in-house.

## CityCircuit Rewards

Rewards members earn **1 point per dollar**. Points are redeemable against
future purchases at **1 cent per point**. A $20 voucher is issued automatically
each time a member reaches 2,000 points.

There is no member pricing tier.

## Delivery

Standard delivery is **$9.95**, free on orders over **$150**. Express delivery
is available at $19.95. Delivery timeframes are estimates only.

## Trade-in

Not offered.

## Repairs

Out-of-warranty repairs are referred to authorised third-party service agents.
CityCircuit does not stock spare parts.
