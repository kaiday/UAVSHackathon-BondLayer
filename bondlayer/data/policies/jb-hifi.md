# JB Hi-Fi — public policy summary

*JB Hi-Fi Australia · collected 13 September 2026 · public-source fixture*

This document paraphrases public pages on `jbhifi.com.au` for catalogue and
agent-evaluation demos. It is not issued, reviewed, or signed by JB Hi-Fi.
Agents should follow the linked source and re-check the live terms before making
a purchasing decision.

## Consumer guarantees and warranty claims

Australian Consumer Law consumer guarantees cannot be excluded. A major product
failure may entitle the customer to a replacement or refund and compensation for
reasonably foreseeable loss. A non-major failure may entitle the customer to a
repair or replacement. These rights have no fixed duration; the reasonable
period depends on matters such as product cost, quality, and representations.

A customer may ask JB Hi-Fi to handle a warranty claim and is not required to go
through the manufacturer. A customer can take a faulty product and proof of
purchase to a store. If the fault cannot be assessed there, the product may be
sent to the manufacturer for assessment. A free return courier may be available,
although this can take longer. Large appliances and some televisions may qualify
for an in-home assessment, collection, and redelivery, with remote-area
exceptions.

## Faulty-product returns

Products bought directly from JB Hi-Fi can be returned to a store with a receipt
or online tax invoice. A customer can instead request a free return courier,
after which the product is assessed before remedies are discussed.

Marketplace products must be returned to the third-party seller. The seller's
policy applies, while the customer's Australian Consumer Law rights continue to
apply.

Source:
- https://support.jbhifi.com.au/hc/en-au/articles/360053783513-How-do-I-return-faulty-goods

## Headphones and selected wearables

For a change-of-mind refund, headphones and some wearables must be completely
unopened, unused, and sealed, and returned within 30 days of purchase. Faulty
items use the standard refund and warranty process.

Source:

## Extra Care

Extra Care is an optional paid cover plan and does not replace Australian
Consumer Law rights.

For products priced at AUD 1,000 or less, Replacement Cover starts 12 months
after delivery or collection and ends three years after delivery or collection,
or when the first replacement or equivalent payment is provided. It provides a
one-off equivalent replacement; if none is available, JB Hi-Fi may provide a
gift card or store credit up to the purchase price.

For products priced above AUD 1,000, Repair Cover starts after the later of the
manufacturer warranty and JB Hi-Fi Minimum Voluntary Warranty. It ends five
years after purchase or delivery, or when the first replacement or equivalent
payment is provided. Full exclusions and limitations remain in the Extra Care
terms.

Source:
- https://www.jbhifi.com.au/pages/extracare

## Delivery and fulfilment

Delivery availability, price, and timing depend on the product and destination
shown at checkout. Smaller media, games, software, and physical gift cards may
use untracked Australia Post mail. Once an online order is submitted, its
delivery choice cannot be edited; the customer must contact JB Hi-Fi for help.

Eligible small-to-medium orders within 20 km of a participating store may show
Uber delivery at checkout. Availability depends on store stock, ordering time,
and item size. The customer receives tracking and the driver asks for the last
four digits of the customer's mobile number. A missed delivery is returned to
the store.

## JB Perks membership

JB Perks is JB Hi-Fi's free membership program. Joining requires a JB Hi-Fi
account and an Australian mobile phone number. Registration also includes
agreement to the JB Perks terms, privacy policy, and JB Hi-Fi marketing and
Perks communications.

New members receive a AUD 10 welcome coupon that is valid for 28 days from
issue. Other published benefits include member-only discounts and offers, early
access to or notification of sales, a birthday perk when the member supplies
their birth month, member-only competitions, and automatic storage of eligible
in-store digital receipts in the member's account.

The public pages do not publish a points-earning rate or a guaranteed dollar
value for birthday perks, discounts, or competitions. Agents should treat those
benefits as conditional and unpriced until a current offer supplies exact terms.

## Price enquiries and price matching

JB Hi-Fi offers a product-and-price enquiry flow through live chat, an online
request, and its sales line. The public support summary does not state a simple,
universal match formula, so an agent should request a quote rather than infer an
automatic discount.
