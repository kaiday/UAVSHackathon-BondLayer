# BIG W — public policy summary

*BIG W Australia · collected 13 September 2026 · public-source fixture*

This document paraphrases public pages on `bigw.com.au` for catalogue and
agent-evaluation demos. It is not issued, reviewed, or signed by BIG W.
Agents should follow the linked source and re-check the live terms before
making a purchasing decision.

## Online and Marketplace orders

BIG W online and app purchases are governed by the BIG W Online, App and
Marketplace Terms and Conditions. Products may be sold by BIG W, fulfilment
partners, or Marketplace sellers. BIG W only delivers to Australian addresses.
Marketplace products are fulfilled by the individual seller.

An order is an offer to purchase at the price, delivery charges, fees, and
taxes shown when the order is placed. BIG W may request current and accurate
customer details and may cancel or refund an order in the circumstances set out
in the terms.

Source:
- https://www.bigw.com.au/your-rights-product-safety/terms-and-conditions

## Returns and faulty products

BIG W states that eligible products changed-of-mind returns can generally be
made within 90 days with proof of purchase. Product-specific exclusions and
Marketplace seller conditions may apply.

For products delivered damaged, defective, or faulty, customers should contact
the BIG W Call Centre on 1300 244 999 or follow the returns instructions
provided with the product. Australian Consumer Law rights continue to apply.

Sources:
- https://www.bigw.com.au/help
- https://www.bigw.com.au/your-rights-product-safety/terms-and-conditions

## Product availability

Technology and other ranges may not be stocked in every store. Online, store,
delivery, and Marketplace availability can vary by location and inventory.

Source:
- https://www.bigw.com.au/your-rights-product-safety/catalogue-terms-conditions

## Everyday Rewards

Everyday Rewards members can generally collect 1 point per AUD 1 spent at BIG W
in store and online, subject to exclusions. Published exclusions include gift
cards, donations, BIG W Photos, BIG W eBay, travel cards and tickets, mobile
recharge, delivery charges, and selected fees or services.

For online orders, the registered Everyday Rewards card must be linked to the
BIG W account before checkout. Points may be reversed when the related goods
are returned or refunded. Member Prices apply only to eligible products marked
with the Member Price indicator and require the member card to be scanned or
linked at checkout.

Source:
- https://www.bigw.com.au/everydayrewards

## Agent handling guidance

Treat price, stock, delivery, Marketplace seller, promotion, and Member Price
information as time-sensitive. Do not promise a return, reward, delivery date,
or promotional point multiplier without checking the current product and offer
terms.
