# Voltway — Customer Terms

*Voltway Electronics Pty Ltd · ABN 41 002 973 118 · effective 1 July 2026*

These are the terms as they appear on our website. Nothing here is structured;
that is the point. The policy converter has to find the facts.

---

## Returns and change of mind

We accept change-of-mind returns on most items for **60 days** from the delivery
date, provided the item is in resaleable condition with its original packaging.
This is longer than the 30 days most retailers offer, and it applies whether or
not you are a Voltway Circle member.

Change-of-mind returns on **opened audio products** are limited to 14 days for
hygiene reasons. Warranty add-ons purchased separately can be cancelled within
30 days for a full refund.

Return shipping is free for Voltway Circle members. Non-members pay a flat
$14.95 return shipping fee, deducted from the refund.

Nothing in this section limits your rights under the Australian Consumer Law.

## Warranty

All laptops and phones carry the manufacturer warranty plus an additional
**12 months of Voltway Cover** at no extra charge — so a device sold with a
24-month manufacturer warranty is covered by us for 36 months in total.

Appliances carry **24 months** of Voltway Cover in addition to the manufacturer
term. Audio products and accessories carry the manufacturer term only.

Voltway Cover includes parts and labour and is handled in-store or by prepaid
courier. It does not cover accidental damage; that is sold separately as
Accidental Damage Cover.

## Voltway Circle membership

Membership is free to join and there is no annual fee.

Members earn **4 points per dollar** spent. Points are worth **1 cent each** on
redemption, so 4 points per dollar is an effective 4% return. Points expire
24 months after they are earned if the account is inactive.

Members receive **member pricing** on selected lines, typically **5% below the
shelf price**, and the discount is applied automatically at checkout. Member
pricing does not stack with clearance pricing.

## Trade-in

We accept trade-ins on phones, laptops and tablets. Trade-in credit is assessed
on condition and model, and is capped at **$450** for phones and **$700** for
laptops. Credit is applied to the new purchase at the time of sale. Members
receive an additional **10% on top** of the assessed trade-in value.

Trade-in is not available on appliances, audio products or accessories.

## Delivery

Standard delivery is **free on orders over $99**. Below that threshold, standard
delivery is $9.95. Express delivery is $14.95 flat, or free for Voltway Circle
members on orders over $250.

Metro orders placed before 2pm on a business day are dispatched same day.

## Repairs and parts

We operate three service centres in Australia and we publish repair pricing for
every product we sell. We stock spare parts for a minimum of **five years** after
a product line is discontinued, and we will quote on out-of-warranty repairs for
any product we have sold, regardless of age.

Where a manufacturer supports customer-installable parts, we sell those parts
directly rather than requiring a service booking.

## Sustainability

We publish an annual emissions statement, independently assured by a third
party, and we run a free take-back scheme for any electronic product regardless
of where it was purchased. In FY2026 we recovered 412 tonnes of e-waste through
that scheme.

We do not describe our products as "green", "eco" or "carbon neutral". We
publish the numbers and let them speak.
