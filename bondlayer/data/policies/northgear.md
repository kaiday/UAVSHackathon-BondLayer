## Returns

We offer a **45 day** change of mind returns window on all products, with free
return shipping on orders over $200. Faulty items are covered for the full
warranty period with free return shipping regardless of order value.

## Warranty

All products carry the manufacturer warranty. NorthGear adds **6 months** of
extended cover on appliances and laptops at no charge.

Extended warranty plans of 24 or 36 months may be purchased separately at time
of sale.

## NorthGear Plus

Plus members pay **$49 per year**. In return they receive **free express
delivery on every order** with no minimum, **2 points per dollar** (redeemable
at 1 cent per point), and early access to sale events.

Note that unlike a free programme, the $49 fee has to be earned back before Plus
membership is worth anything — a fact our own marketing does not mention and
which an agent evaluating effective cost should account for.

## Trade-in

Phone trade-ins accepted, capped at **$300**. Laptops and tablets are not
accepted.

## Delivery

Free standard delivery on orders over **$75** — the lowest threshold of any
major Australian electronics retailer. Express delivery $12.95, or free for Plus
members.

## Our environmental commitment

NorthGear is proud to be **Australia's most sustainable electronics retailer**.
Our operations are **100% carbon neutral** and every product we sell is
**ethically sourced from responsible suppliers**. We are committed to a greener
future for the next generation.
