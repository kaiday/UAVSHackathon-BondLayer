JB Hihi — Demo Store Policies
Effective date: 27 September 2026

DEMO ONLY
These fictional policies are prepared for the BondLayer demo. JB Hihi is a
fictional merchant and is not affiliated with JB Hi-Fi. These terms are not
statements of any real retailer's policies.

1. CHANGE-OF-MIND RETURNS
Customers may return laptops, phones, audio products and accessories within
30 calendar days of delivery for a refund of the product's purchase price.
Products must be undamaged, reset to factory settings where applicable, and
returned with all included accessories and packaging. Opened products are
accepted if they meet these conditions. Proof of purchase is required.
JB Hihi provides a prepaid return label for one approved return per order
within Australia. The customer pays no return postage for that return.
Refunds are made to the original payment method within 5 business days after
the returned product has been received and inspected. Original delivery fees
are not refunded for change-of-mind returns.

2. ADDITIONAL STORE WARRANTY
JB Hihi provides a 12-month store warranty from the delivery date for laptops,
phones, headphones, speakers and powered accessories purchased from JB Hihi.
The warranty covers manufacturing defects under normal use. It excludes
accidental damage, liquid damage, misuse and normal battery wear.
JB Hihi pays for return postage for an approved warranty claim within Australia.
The remedy is repair or replacement without a service charge under this
additional warranty. It does not restrict rights under Australian Consumer Law.

3. DELIVERY
Standard delivery within Australia is free when the order's product subtotal
is AUD 99 or more, after discounts and before delivery fees.
For an order below AUD 99, standard delivery costs AUD 9.95 per order.
Standard delivery takes an estimated 3 to 7 business days after dispatch.
These delivery terms do not cover international deliveries or express delivery.

4. REPAIR SUPPORT
For laptops and phones purchased from JB Hihi, customers can request a repair
assessment for up to 3 years after the purchase date.
JB Hihi provides a written quote before any paid out-of-warranty repair starts.
The customer must approve the quote before work starts. Out-of-warranty repairs
are paid services; this policy does not promise free repairs or parts availability.

5. LOYALTY POINTS
Membership in the JB Hihi Rewards demo programme is free.
An identified member earns 2 points per AUD 1 of product spend after discounts,
excluding delivery charges. Each point has a redemption value of AUD 0.01.
Points are credited 30 days after delivery and expire 12 months after crediting.
Points from refunded purchases are reversed. Points are not redeemable for cash.
Redemption requires an identified member account and an available points balance.

6. TRADE-IN
An eligible customer may receive up to AUD 200 of credit when trading in a
working laptop towards a new laptop purchased from JB Hihi.
The actual credit depends on model, condition and inspection. AUD 200 is a
maximum, not a guaranteed amount. One trade-in is allowed per new laptop order.
The customer must own the device and erase their personal data before handover.
Trade-in eligibility and value must be confirmed before credit is applied.

7. CONTACT
For this fictional demo, submit policy questions using the merchant console.
No real customer support address or external claim is supplied in this file.
