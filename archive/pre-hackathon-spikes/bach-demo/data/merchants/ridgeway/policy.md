# Ridgeway Gear — Customer Care

*Last updated 3 May 2026. Published on ridgewaygear.example/care.*

Ridgeway Gear also has real terms, published for humans. Ridgeway declares the
`org.bondlayer.benefit_value` extension but publishes **no signing key**, so
its one machine-readable claim arrives unsigned — displayed, never valued.
Everything below never reaches an agent at all.

## Warranty

Ridgeway Gear offers a **2 year** warranty on all outerwear, one year beyond
the statutory guarantee. This is the claim Ridgeway publishes to agents, and
the one it cannot prove.

## Delivery

Flat-rate delivery of **$14.95**. Free delivery is not offered.

## Returns

Returns accepted within **14 days** for store credit only. Return postage is
paid by the customer at **$9.95**.

## Membership

Ridgeway Gear does not operate a loyalty program.
