# Peak Supply Co — Shipping & Returns

*Last updated 12 June 2026. Published on peaksupply.example/help — written for
customers, like almost every merchant policy page on the web.*

Peak Supply Co is not a worse merchant than its competitors. It has real,
competitive terms, and it publishes them clearly — **for humans**. It has no
BondLayer, no UCP extension and no signing key, so none of the below is on the
wire when a shopping agent asks. That is the entire point of the demo: the gap
is legibility, not merit.

## Delivery

Standard delivery is **$9.95** Australia-wide, and **free on orders over $99**.
Orders placed before noon are dispatched the same business day.

## Returns

We accept change-of-mind returns within **30 days** of delivery, provided the
item is unworn with tags attached. Return postage is paid by the customer at
**$9.95** unless the item is faulty.

## Warranty

All jackets carry a **12 month** manufacturer warranty against defects. This
matches the statutory guarantee under Australian Consumer Law, so it confers
no advantage beyond the legal minimum.

## Membership

Peak Supply Co does not operate a loyalty program.
