# Alpine Outfitters — Customer Policy & Summit Club Terms

*Last updated 1 July 2026. This page is written for customers, not machines —
which is the point. It is the raw input to the policy converter (D7).*

## Returns

We accept returns on unworn items with tags attached for **60 days** from the
delivery date. Alpine Outfitters pays return postage on all orders — you will
never be charged the **$9.95** return label fee to send something back.
Refunds are processed to the original payment method within five business days
of the item reaching our warehouse.

Sale items may be returned for store credit only.

## Warranty

Every Alpine Outfitters hardshell carries a **24 month** manufacturer warranty
against defects in materials and workmanship. This is in addition to your
rights under Australian Consumer Law, which provides a statutory guarantee for
a period of 12 months.

## Delivery

Standard delivery is **$12.95**, and free on orders over $100. Orders placed
before 2pm on a business day are dispatched the same day.

## Summit Club membership

Summit Club is free to join. Members earn **2 points per dollar** spent.
Points are redeemable against future purchases at a rate of **100 points = $1**.
Points expire 24 months after they are earned.

Gold tier members — customers who have spent $600 or more in the preceding 12
months — receive an additional **5% off the list price** of all full-price
items, and early access to seasonal sales.

Members move to Platinum tier at $1,200 of annual spend. Platinum members
receive free express delivery and an extended returns window.
