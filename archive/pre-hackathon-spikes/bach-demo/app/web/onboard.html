Step 3 of 4

    
Your policy, turned into claims an agent can price

    
This is the one step where a model is genuinely necessary —
    nobody hand-codes every retailer's terms. It is also where a mistake would
    do the most damage, so the model is allowed to draft and nothing
    else. It has no access to a signing key.


    


    

      
Read the quotes, not our summary

      
Every claim below must cite the sentence in your document it came
      from. A quote your document does not contain is the clearest sign the
      model invented something, and it is flagged on the record rather than
      quietly dropped. Anything flagged is unticked by default — your
      job on the next screen is to decide.

Step 4 of 4

    
Nothing is published until you say so

    
The guardrail is structural, not statistical.
    sign_approved() only ever receives records a person ticked; an
    unapproved draft has no signature, and no agent will value an unsigned
    claim. Untick anything you are not willing to stand behind.
