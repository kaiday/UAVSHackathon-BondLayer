Voltway — Customer Commitments

Warranty
All Voltway-branded chargers, power banks and peripherals carry a 24-month
replacement warranty from date of purchase. This is 12 months beyond the
statutory minimum. Replacement is like-for-like and does not require the
original packaging.

Delivery
Free standard shipping on every order over $25. Standard delivery is 2-4
business days within Australia.

Returns
Change-of-mind returns accepted within 60 days, unopened or opened, with
return postage paid by Voltway.

Repairability
Voltway publishes spare-part diagrams for all powered products and stocks
replacement cells for 5 years after a product is discontinued.

Loyalty
Voltway Circle members earn 5% back as store credit on every order. Store
credit is redeemable at Voltway only and expires 12 months after it is issued.
Membership is free and does not require a subscription. New Circle members
receive 10% off their first order, with no minimum spend.
